The artist

Rock with a melodic edge.

Patrick Lang is an independent rock artist from Germany, combining the energy and attitude of 1980s melodic rock and hard rock with catchy hooks and modern production.

Writing lyrics and songs, performing lead vocals, and shaping the arrangements and production, Patrick brings together classic rock influences and a contemporary independent approach.
